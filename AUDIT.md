# Nyx final audit — 2026-10-02

This is an evidence log, not a certificate of App Store or accessibility compliance. Device interaction gates remain open because no physical device was used for interaction testing. Several four-second iOS 27 captures initially caught launch/count-up frames; these were recaptured after eight seconds, and incomplete frames are not evidence. Simulator screenshots establish visible layout; they do not establish VoiceOver focus order, physical frame rate, glare, haptics or on-device model behavior.

## Design critique and repairs

The review uses [Apple's dark-mode guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode), [accessibility guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility), and the restraint/platform integration visible in [Apple Design Award winners](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/). Nyx keeps system navigation, glass tabs, sheets, search, form controls and permission dialogs. Canvas work carries the celestial identity.

First checkpoint's three weakest details were park identity below the hero score, an opaque silhouette in the moon-phase tab, and unreadable compact calendar cells at AX5. Repairs put the winning park above the gauge, render only the illuminated phase as the tab icon, and substitute a full date/score list at accessibility text sizes.

Final pass's three weakest details were access guidance below the first viewport, pale-red native UI with weak secondary labels, and dates/reminders that could drift with travel or asynchronous updates. Repairs move access guidance before the Tonight gauge, shorten the heading, render every native/custom surface in signal red with readable secondary copy, apply the theme to sheet hosts, format journal/update dates in each park's zone, and schedule/remove notifications immediately when preferences change. A final native-control critique also corrected filled-button labels, toggle thumb/track separation and Stepper's tertiary background. Black text on amber/red actions and darker control fills preserve contrast through the monochrome filter. Onboarding's illustrative score is explicitly labeled. Calendar breakdown can share its actual night.

The resulting visual language is coherent: warm serif scores, seeded restrained stars, physically correct phase geometry, hollow unknown-forecast marks, and honest polar/access caveats. The moon remains a deliberately quiet vector sphere rather than borrowed imagery. Award-level interaction quality still needs dark-field testing with real people; screenshot appeal alone is insufficient.

The matrix is representative, not a claim that every OS-owned state was exercised.

### Contrast

`python3 Scripts/contrast.py` computes WCAG relative luminance for the design tokens; results live in `Research/contrast.json`. Primary/secondary on the normal panel: 16.66:1 / 8.89:1. Signal-red primary/secondary on the representative dark form panel: 5.62:1 / 5.26:1; red on black: 6.16:1. Filled action labels are black: 11.91:1 on amber, 6.16:1 on signal red. Red thumb against the dark track token is 4.95:1. These pass the 4.5:1 body-text gate. Token calculations do not prove every antialiased, disabled or composited native pixel; device sampling of native forms/glass remains required. Users are advised to lower screen brightness; the app does not claim to preserve night adaptation at maximum brightness.

## Performance evidence and limits

Starfield is capped at 88 particles, gauge at 12 orbit particles, decorative timelines at 20 Hz; Canvas remains separate from native interaction rendering. Low Power Mode pauses decorative timelines. Reduce Motion makes the rendering static. Moon uses 160 strips; sky paths use 65 samples each. Astronomy conditions are cached per park/local evening. Cached forecast coverage uses deterministic hourly integration. No network blocks a core screen.

The first Instruments attachment ended when the screenshot runner relaunched its target; that 0.69-second trace is invalid performance evidence. A later iOS 27 simulator run stalled installation/profiling while its system services (chronod, backboardd, speech and location services) consumed high CPU. Subsequent sequential Time Profiler and Allocations captures completed. The 15.794-second active-decoration detail trace recorded zero >250 ms hangs; raw allocations are locally retained for Instruments review. Native UI smoke tests pass on both runtimes; XCTest launch measurements and CPU benchmark results are in `Research/performance.md`. Do not report simulator-environment stalls as app hangs or infer 60/120 fps from particle counts. Physical checks remain in INPUT_NEEDED.

## Physical review procedure — release gate

1. On physical iPhones running iOS 26 and 27, install the final signed TestFlight build. Also use an older supported iPhone for the decorative frame budget. Record model, build, OS, refresh rate, brightness and battery state. Do not claim an iPhone 12 runtime if the OS cannot run on it; use the oldest actually supported hardware and record the brief's device mismatch.
2. Complete onboarding without permissions, choose a manual park, deny location, widen radius, search/filter/save a park, visit polar/tropical dates, scrub the river, change months/parks, share an actual night, create/edit/delete a journal with four selected photos, kill/relaunch and confirm persistence. Repeat in airplane mode and with both data toggles off. Test stale/failed updates and actual NPS closures once configured.
3. Repeat each screen and sheet with VoiceOver, AX5, Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, Bold Text and Smart Invert individually, then AX5+Bold Text and red+contrast together. Check focus order, labels, score/caveat announcements, touch target sizes, modal dismissal, date/park pickers, keyboard, PhotosPicker and ShareLink. Fix clipping or unnamed elements before sign-off.
4. Install all four widget families and the Control Center toggle; change saved parks and night mode, confirm refresh/deep links, and ask the Siri shortcut for an unsaved park. Verify unknown clouds are spoken. Check real local notification authorization, delivery and removal; disabling reminders must prevent late additions.
5. On a model-available iPhone, ask supported/unsupported questions, try injected instructions in notes/questions, cancel streaming, request a recap and Learn explanation, and confirm visible citations refer only to supplied records. Original data remains the authority. On unavailable devices no AI entry point should appear.
6. Use Instruments on-device for launch, scrolling, scrubbing, allocations, animation hitches and thermal/Low Power behavior. Inspect native glass over stars and native forms after the red filter. Spend time outside with night-adapted eyes. Give the app to two or three park visitors, including a VoiceOver user where possible, and fix observed failures.
7. Record every outcome here with evidence. All unverified gates are blockers for claiming complete accessibility or award-level interaction, even though the core builds and simulator tests pass.
