# Input needed: the steps left for Nyx 1.1, in order

Build 1.1 (7) is archived, verified and uploaded (it supersedes 1.1 (6) with the UI and UX review fixes); the visionOS build still waits for the platform to be added; screenshots for every device are in `Store/`. Each step below needs your Apple ID, your accounts, your money or your own eyes on real hardware. Do them top to bottom. Every paste-ready field is in `SUBMISSION.md` and `Store/1.1/`.

1. **Try Nyx on your iPhone, ideally at night.** *Why only you:* the simulator has no Taptic Engine, compass, brightness, alarms, Lock Screen, widget host or real sky. *How:* plug the iPhone in, choose it as the run destination in Xcode, press Run (or install the 1.1 TestFlight build after step 7). Check, and note anything odd for a fix:
   - Core: VoiceOver on the gauge, time river and calendar; haptics while scrubbing the river; night vision from Control Center; the Tonight widget on the Home and Lock Screen; "Hey Siri, what is the darkness score at Joshua Tree in Nyx"; "Near me" with location allowed and denied; adding journal photos; Airplane Mode. (The automated accessibility audit already passes on every screen in the simulator; this is the human pass.)
   - Field mode (needs a real sky): "I'm here tonight" on a park's detail; the screen dims and stays awake; "Where to look" puts the Moon and planets where they are and says true or magnetic north honestly; the dark-adaptation notice after leaving Nyx a while; "Wake me" alarms ring through Silent; the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown runs and ends at dawn; the Control Center "Field mode" control; a Focus with the Stargazing filter turns night vision on and off.
   - Sound and touch: Tonight → Settings → Accessibility → Sound and touch, "Feel a new/half/full Moon" and "Play tonight" with Silent on and off and with headphones; on the river, each night ticks firmer on high scores; with VoiceOver, the rotor's Audio Graph on the river, a calendar month and the shape of the night; the calendar "Best nights" and Parks "Closures" rotors; on iOS 27, "Bortle" said as BOR-tl. Voice Control: "Tap River", "Tap 17" on the calendar, "Tap Listen".
   - Platform surfaces: the large and medium Tonight's sky widgets with two or three saved parks ("2 of 3 ›"); the small widget in StandBy at night (red, legible); the widget in a Smart Stack around dusk on a Good night; Siri "Find the best night at Joshua Tree with Nyx" with "Next best" and "Open in Nyx"; Spotlight "dark sky park in Utah"; on iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence on, Ask Nyx "Which weekend in November is best near Arches?" lists what it looked up; a day after first use, About the data shows "How dark Nyx keeps your screen" (MetricKit, device only).
   - Delight: the alternate app icons (Settings → Appearance → App icon) on the Home Screen in dark, tinted and clear modes; first light at a park in true darkness; Add to Calendar from the trip planner.
   - Record pass or fail with the build number in `AUDIT.md`. This pass also decides which Accessibility Nutrition Labels you may declare (`Store/1.1/accessibility-nutrition-labels.md` § Device pass): VoiceOver, Voice Control, Larger Text, grayscale (Differentiate Without Color), contrast sampling, Reduce Motion.

2. **Try the Apple Watch app.** *Why only you:* complications, the Smart Stack and red on a real wrist cannot be judged in a simulator. *How:* with your Watch paired, run the **Nyx** scheme on the iPhone; the watch app installs with it (its bundle IDs are already registered and carry the App Group). At night check Tonight (turn the Digital Crown through the three pages), the dark-adaptation countdown, Parks → Colors, and the complications on a face (circular, corner, rectangular, inline) and in the Smart Stack. Saving a park on the iPhone should show up on the watch with clouds; red should stay comfortable with dark-adapted eyes.

3. **Try iPad, and Vision Pro if you can.** *Why only you:* hardware keyboard, pointer, Stage Manager and headset brightness can't be simulated. *How:* iPad: the sidebar, Parks beside the park, rotate in "Where to look" (the sky stays upright), ⌘1–⌘5, ⌘F, ⌘← ⌘→ on a park or the calendar, a pointer over the gauge and river, two Nyx windows, the extra-large widget, a narrow window (falls back to the iPhone layout). Vision Pro: run the **NyxVision** scheme; star and Moon brightness were tuned on simulator captures, so check them in a dark room.

4. **Have a native Spanish speaker review Nyx in Spanish.** *Why only you:* a machine draft needs a fluent human before it carries the app's voice, and only you can find and pay a reviewer (ideally a Mexican or US-Hispanic stargazer or park worker). *How:* put an iPhone in Spanish (Settings → General → Language & Region → Español (México)), or run the simulator with `-AppleLanguages "(es)" -AppleLocale es_MX`; reference captures are `Research/Screenshots/es-*-27.png` and the ten store frames in `Store/Framed/es/6.9-inch/`. Edit only `Research/localization/` and run `python3 Scripts/apply_translations.py` (never the `.xcstrings` by hand). Check first:
   - `es-strings.json`: the uncertain choices listed in `Research/localization/README.md` (*Prístina*, *Mala*, *Sin cruce*, *Queda oscuridad total por*, *Primera luz*, *Noches afuera*, *Concentración* / *modo Silencio* and other iOS UI names, *sin escalones*, *Hawaiʻi*), the iPad strings (*Buscar un parque*, *Noche siguiente* / *Noche anterior*, *Mostrar en Calendario*, *Abrir el %@ en %@*, *Donde el cielo es más oscuro*), access copy (*Sin acceso por carretera*, *Parques a los que llegas en auto*, *Cómo llegar: %@*), the 19 sentences that end with a time and no period (*Sin Luna de %@ a %@*; see the glossary's Times note), and the viewing-spot caveat added 2026-10-06 (*Coordenadas aproximadas. Confirma con un guardaparques…*, *La Unidad Norte usa la hora del Centro…*).
   - `es-access-notes.json` (17 park access notes), `es-infoplist.json` (*este dispositivo*), `learn-es/*.md` (six essays), `es-shower-names.json`, `es-vision-strings.json`.
   - Store copy: `Store/1.1/metadata-es.md` and the Spanish screenshot captions in `Scripts/make_store_frames.swift` (`spanishFrames`); the Spanish description should gain the 1.1 features (Watch, Vision Pro, trip planner, sky glow) during the review. If captions change, run `swift Scripts/make_store_frames.swift es`.
   - Needed before the Spanish localization is submitted. If the review isn't done in time, submit 1.1 in English only and add Spanish metadata later (the app's Spanish UI still ships).

5. **Publish the updated privacy page.** *Why only you:* it publishes to the public site. *Why it matters:* Nyx 1.1 contacts a third host, `air-quality-api.open-meteo.com` (smoke and haze, behind its own switch), and the store's privacy link must say so before review. `docs/privacy.html`, `support.html` and `index.html` already do. *How:* merge `roadmap` into `main` and `git push origin main`; check https://harrypakhale98.github.io/Nyx/privacy.html mentions air quality.

6. **Set up the 1.1 version in App Store Connect.** *Why only you:* the account holder's login. *How* (details in `SUBMISSION.md`):
   - If 1.0 (5) was never submitted, rename the pending version to 1.1; otherwise + Version → 1.1.
   - **Mac Availability:** Pricing and Availability → uncheck "Make this app available on Mac" (an iPad app is otherwise offered on Apple silicon Macs; Nyx was never tried there).
   - **visionOS:** the app → + Add Platform → visionOS (same bundle ID, universal purchase).
   - Add the **Spanish (Mexico)** localization (after step 4).

7. **Upload build 1.1 (7) and the visionOS build.** *Why only you:* distribution signing and upload use your account's certificates. *How:* Xcode → Window → Organizer → Archives. If `Nyx 1.1 (7)` and `NyxVision 1.1 (7)` are not listed (the archives live in `/tmp`; double-click `/tmp/Nyx-1.1-7.xcarchive` to add it, or Product → Archive again with the **Nyx** scheme, Any iOS Device, then the **NyxVision** scheme, Any visionOS Device). Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload, with automatic signing. Answer export compliance: standard HTTPS only (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO`). Then install from TestFlight and repeat the quick checks from step 1 on the distributed build (iOS 26 and 27 if you have both).
   - **Done 2026-10-06:** iPhone/iPad build 1.1 (6), with the Watch app inside, was exported with App Store signing and uploaded to App Store Connect ("Upload succeeded"). It appears in TestFlight once Apple finishes processing (usually 10–30 minutes; you'll get an email); answer the export-compliance question there if asked (the app declares no non-exempt encryption). The visionOS build is not uploaded yet: add the visionOS platform to the app record first, then upload `/tmp/NyxVision-1.1-7.xcarchive` from Organizer (or ask me).
   - **Done 2026-10-06:** build 1.1 (7) (the UI and UX review: search at accessibility sizes on iOS 27, disclosure rows, onboarding, gauge, reading comfort), with the Watch app inside, exported with App Store signing and uploaded ("Upload succeeded"). It appears in TestFlight after processing; answer export compliance there if asked. Build 6 stays in TestFlight; submit 7.

8. **Enter the metadata and screenshots.** *How:* paste from `Store/1.1/metadata.md` (English) and `metadata-es.md` (Spanish): What's New, promotional text, description (remove "Voice Control" unless step 1 confirmed it), keywords, subtitle `The night sky, park by park`. Screenshots, replacing every earlier upload: iPhone 6.9" `Store/Framed/6.9-inch/` (ten, in file order) and Spanish `Store/Framed/es/6.9-inch/`; optional 6.5" from the matching `6.5-inch/` folders; iPad 13" `Store/Screenshots/iPad/` (six); Apple Watch `Store/Screenshots/Watch/` (six, 422×514, the same size for every language); Vision Pro `Store/Screenshots/Vision/` (four, 3840×2160). 9. **Answer App Privacy and Accessibility.** App Privacy: "No, we do not collect data from this app" (Data Not Collected), no tracking; reasoning in `PRIVACY.md`, third host explained in `SUBMISSION.md` § Privacy. Accessibility Nutrition Labels: declare per device only what step 1 confirmed (table in `SUBMISSION.md`). Re-answer the age rating (unchanged answers).

10. **Ask NPS for a higher request limit for the Nyx key** (optional before launch, needed if Nyx grows). *Why only you:* only the key's owner can ask. Every install shares the one key; NPS keys default to about 1,000 requests an hour. Nyx sends at most one alerts request per park every ten minutes and keeps the last known alerts if the limit is reached. *How:* the contact link on developer.nps.gov; name the key and describe the use (a free app reading park alerts and night-sky events).

11. **In-App Events and the featuring nomination.** *Why only you:* account forms with Apple review. *How:* `Store/1.1/in-app-events.md` (eleven events; the 16:9 card and 9:16 detail images are ready in `Store/1.1/events/`, rendered from the app by `Scripts/capture_events.py` + `Scripts/make_event_media.swift`; submit each at least 7 days before its publish date) and `Store/1.1/featuring-nominations.md`: **App Enhancements (Nyx 1.1)** by about **Oct 28** for a mid-November release (three weeks' lead), the Geminids event nomination by **Nov 20**. App Launch only if 1.0 never shipped.
