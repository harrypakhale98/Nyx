# Input needed — the only steps left, in order

Everything else is built, verified and documented. Each step below needs your Apple ID, your accounts, or your own eyes on a real iPhone.

1. **Try Nyx on your iPhone, ideally at night.** Plug it in, pick it as the run destination in Xcode, press Run. Check: VoiceOver on the gauge, time river and calendar; haptics while scrubbing the river; night vision from Control Center; the Tonight widget on the Home and Lock Screen; "Hey Siri, what is the darkness score at Joshua Tree in Nyx"; "Near me" with location allowed and denied; adding journal photos; Airplane Mode. The automated accessibility audit already passes on every screen in the simulator (`NyxUITests/AccessibilityAuditTests.swift`); this is the human pass. Field mode needs a real iPhone under a real sky, since the simulator has no compass, brightness, alarms or Lock Screen: open "I'm here tonight" on a park's detail and check that the screen dims and stays awake, "Where to look" points the Moon and planets where they are (and says true or magnetic north honestly), the dark-adaptation notice appears after leaving Nyx for a while, "Wake me" alarms ring through Silent, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown runs and ends at dawn, the Control Center "Field mode" control opens it, and a Focus with the Stargazing filter (night vision switched on) turns night vision on and back off when the Focus ends. Sound and touch (only a real iPhone has a Taptic Engine and a speaker worth judging): in Tonight → Settings → Accessibility → Sound and touch, try "Feel a new/half/full Moon" and "Play tonight", with Silent on and off and with headphones; scrub the time river and feel each night tick firmer on high scores and the Moon's texture when you rest; with VoiceOver, open the rotor on the time river, a calendar month title and the shape of the night and choose Audio Graph, try the calendar's "Best nights" and the Parks "Closures" rotors, and on iOS 27 listen for "Bortle" said as BOR-tl on a park's detail. Voice Control: say "Tap River", "Tap 17" on the calendar, "Tap Listen". Platform surfaces (2026-10-06; the simulator cannot host these): add the large Tonight's sky widget and a medium one to the Home Screen with two or three saved parks and tap "2 of 3 ›"; put the small widget in StandBy and check it at night (red) is legible; put the widget in a Smart Stack and see whether it rises around dusk on a Good night; ask Siri "Find the best night at Joshua Tree with Nyx" and try the snippet's "Next best" and "Open in Nyx"; search Spotlight for "dark sky park in Utah"; on iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence on, ask Nyx "Which weekend in November is best near Arches?" and check it lists what it looked up; a day after first use, check About the data shows "How dark Nyx keeps your screen" (MetricKit reports arrive about daily, and only on a real device). Note anything odd and ask for a fix.

2. **Publish on the App Store** (follow `SUBMISSION.md`, which has every field filled in):
   - App Store Connect → My Apps → + New App: iOS, name **Nyx: Dark Sky Planner** ("Nyx" is taken), bundle ID `com.harrypakhale.nyx`, SKU `nyx-ios`.
   - Paste the description, keywords, subtitle and URLs from `SUBMISSION.md`. If you entered the description before, paste it again; it gained two sentences.
   - App Privacy: "No, we do not collect data from this app". Reasoning is in `PRIVACY.md`.
   - Upload the six 6.9-inch screenshots in `Store/Screenshots/` (no alpha channel), replacing any uploaded earlier.
   - **Build 1.0 (5) is uploaded** (2026-10-05 design pass: score readout, week strip, fact-based breakdown, journal sky; builds 1–4 are superseded). When it finishes processing: test it in TestFlight, select it on the version page, then Submit for Review.
   - Upload the six captioned frames in `Store/Framed/6.9-inch/` (and `6.5-inch/`), recaptured 2026-10-05, replacing earlier ones.

3. **Ask NPS for a higher request limit for the Nyx key** (optional before launch, needed if Nyx grows). Every install shares the one key, and NPS keys default to about 1,000 requests an hour. Nyx now sends only alerts for most screens, at most one request per park every ten minutes, but a popular launch could still reach the limit; Nyx then quietly keeps the last known alerts and says when they were checked. Use the contact link on developer.nps.gov, name the key, and describe the use (a free app reading park alerts and night-sky events). Only the key's owner can ask.

4. **Enter the awards that close soon** (only the account owner can enter and pay). The full plan is in `Research/award-roadmap.md` (Tier 4). Once 1.0 is live: file an App Launch featuring nomination in App Store Connect (app → Featuring → Nominations); enter the **Webby Awards** by the early deadline **Oct 30, 2026** (Apps, Software & Immersive); register for the **iF Design Award** by **Nov 4, 2026** (UI/UX). Fees and later deadlines are in the roadmap.

6. **Before the first build with the Apple Watch app goes to App Store Connect** (it ships inside the iOS app, roadmap 1.2):
   - Signing: open the project in Xcode once, signed in with the team account, and build the **Nyx** scheme for your iPhone with your Apple Watch paired. Automatic signing registers `com.harrypakhale.nyx.watchkitapp` and `com.harrypakhale.nyx.watchkitapp.widgets` and adds both to the App Group `group.com.harrypakhale.nyx`. If Xcode reports a capability error instead, add the App Group to those two identifiers at developer.apple.com → Identifiers. Only the account holder can do this.
   - Try it at night: on the watch, Tonight (turn the Digital Crown through the three pages), the dark-adaptation countdown, Parks → Colors, and add the Nyx complications to a face (circular, corner, rectangular, inline) and the Smart Stack. Check that saving a park on the iPhone shows up on the watch with clouds, and that red stays comfortable with dark-adapted eyes.
   - App Store Connect requires Apple Watch screenshots for a version that contains a watch app: capture them from the **NyxWatch** scheme on an Apple Watch simulator (Ultra and Series sizes) or the watch itself, and upload them under the version's Apple Watch section.

7. **Put Nyx on Apple Vision Pro in the App Store** (when you want to ship the visionOS app; not needed for the iPhone release). The `NyxVision` target already uses the iPhone app's bundle ID `com.harrypakhale.nyx`, so it joins the existing "Nyx: Dark Sky Planner" record as one universal purchase. In App Store Connect: open the app → **+ (Add Platform)** → visionOS; archive the **NyxVision** scheme (Product → Archive, destination Any visionOS Device) and upload; add visionOS screenshots (captures in `Research/Screenshots/vision-*`); submit the visionOS version. Try it on a headset first if you can: star and Moon sizes can't be judged in the simulator.

8. **Before the first build that runs on iPad goes to App Store Connect** (the `ipad` work, 2026-10-06; only the account owner can change availability, upload screenshots or hold an iPad):
   - **iPad screenshots are required** once a build supports iPad: at least one 13-inch iPad image (2064×2752 portrait or 2752×2064 landscape, checked on Apple's specification page 2026-10-06). Six are ready in `Store/Screenshots/iPad/` (no alpha). In App Store Connect → the version → Previews and Screenshots → iPad 13-inch, upload them.
   - **Mac availability:** an iPad app is offered on Apple silicon Macs ("Designed for iPad") unless you opt out. Nyx was not tried on a Mac, and field mode, the compass, location, alarms and Live Activities do not apply there. Recommended: App Store Connect → the app → Pricing and Availability → **Mac Availability** → uncheck "Make this app available on Mac" (choose "Don't make this app available"). Revisit after running the **Nyx** scheme with the "My Mac (Designed for iPad)" destination.
   - **Try it on an iPad if you have one:** the sidebar (swipe or the sidebar button), Parks beside the park, rotate in field mode's "Where to look" (the sky should stay upright in landscape), a keyboard (⌘1–⌘5, ⌘F, ⌘← ⌘→ on a park or the calendar), a pointer over the gauge and the time river, two Nyx windows side by side, the extra-large Tonight's sky widget on the Home Screen, and Stage Manager or a narrow window (it should fall back to the iPhone layout).

9. **Have a native Spanish speaker review Nyx in Spanish before the 1.1 release** (the app now ships an `es` localization; only you can find and pay a reviewer, ideally a Mexican or US-Hispanic stargazer or park worker). Why only you: a machine-drafted translation needs a fluent human's judgement before it carries the app's honest voice. How: put an iPhone in Spanish (Settings → General → Language & Region → add Español (México) or Español (EE. UU.)), or run the simulator with `-AppleLanguages "(es)" -AppleLocale es_MX`; the 19 reference captures are `Research/Screenshots/es-*-27.png`. Edit `Research/localization/es-strings.json` (and the other files named below), then run `python3 Scripts/apply_translations.py`; never edit the `.xcstrings` Spanish by hand. Strings to check first, by file:
   - `Research/localization/es-strings.json`: the uncertain choices and long labels listed in `Research/localization/README.md` ("strings flagged for native review"): *Prístina*, *Mala*, *Sin cruce*, *Queda oscuridad total por*, *Primera luz*, *Noches afuera*, *Concentración* / *modo Silencio* and the other iOS UI names, *sin escalones*, *Hawaiʻi*; the eight added 2026-10-06 (*Buscar un parque*, *Noche siguiente* / *Noche anterior*, *Mostrar en Calendario*, *Abrir el %@ en %@*, *Aquí se abrirán su cielo de esta noche y las noches siguientes.*, *Donde el cielo es más oscuro*, *Revisión del widget extragrande*); access copy (*Sin acceso por carretera*, *Parques a los que llegas en auto*, *Cómo llegar: %@*); and the 16 sentences that now end with a time and no period (*Sin Luna hasta las %@*: see the glossary's Times note).
   - `Research/localization/es-access-notes.json`: the 17 park access notes (*Solo se llega en barco…*, *taxi aéreo*, *zonas remotas*, *barcaza para autos*, *reservación*).
   - `Research/localization/es-infoplist.json`: the two permission strings now say *este dispositivo* (iPhone and iPad share one Info.plist).
   - `Research/localization/learn-es/*.md`: the six Learn essays (*Un cielo para todos* is a free title).
   - `Research/localization/es-shower-names.json` and `es-vision-strings.json`: shower names and the Vision Pro copy (*Entrar en este cielo*, *En el cielo toda la noche*).
   - `Store/1.1/metadata-es.md`: the Spanish (Mexico) App Store name, subtitle, description and keywords.
   Needed before the build carrying Spanish is submitted for review.
