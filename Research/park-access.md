# Park access notes

Planning notes for parks where the night sky is not a simple drive away, or where road access has limits worth knowing. Each note rests on the park's official nps.gov page, retrieved 2026-10-06. Conditions, seasons and transport schedules change; check with the park before you go.

Machine-readable copy: `Research/park-access.json` (merged into `parks.json` by `Scripts/build_parks.py`). Spanish: `Research/localization/es-access-notes.json`.

`road` = yes when a private car can reach a place in or at the park from which to watch the night sky.

| Park | Note | Road | Source | Retrieved | NPS basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `chis` | Reached only by boat from Ventura or Santa Barbara. Seeing the night sky means camping on an island. | no | https://www.nps.gov/chis/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Islands can only be reached by boat.” Camping page: campgrounds are the only overnight option. |
| `drto` | Boat or seaplane only. Seeing the night sky means camping on Garden Key, reached by ferry or private boat. | no | https://www.nps.gov/drto/planyourvisit/camping.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Visitors can only reach Dry Tortugas by boat or seaplane.” Camping page: “The seaplane does not offer transport for campers.” |
| `isro` | Ferry or seaplane only, mid-May to September. Closed Nov 1 to Apr 15. Night skies mean staying on the island. | no | https://www.nps.gov/isro/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “The ferries and seaplane provide transportation for the public from the middle of May until September.” “Closed to all visitors from November 1 until April 15.” |
| `gaar` | No roads enter the park. Fly in by air taxi, or hike in from the Dalton Highway, and camp in the backcountry. | no | https://www.nps.gov/gaar/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Visitors must fly or hike into the park”; hiking in from the Dalton Highway or Anaktuvuk Pass is the alternative to air taxis. |
| `kova` | No roads reach the park. Fly from Kotzebue or Bettles by air taxi; the night sky means backcountry camping. | no | https://www.nps.gov/kova/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “There are no roads to provide access, so planes take care of most transportation needs.” Basic info: no roads or campgrounds. |
| `lacl` | Not on the road system; most visitors arrive by small plane. Overnight means a lodge or backcountry camping. | no | https://www.nps.gov/lacl/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Lake Clark is not on the road system; therefore, access is primarily by small aircraft.” Lodging ranges from rustic camping to lodges. |
| `katm` | Reached by small plane or boat from King Salmon. Brooks Camp campground and lodge run June 1 to Sept 17. | no | https://www.nps.gov/katm/planyourvisit/brooks-camp.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Most people arrive at Brooks Camp via small, float-equipped aircraft.” Campground and services run June 1 to September 17 (directions page: access from King Salmon by small aircraft or water taxi). |
| `glba` | No road leads to Glacier Bay. Fly or ferry to Gustavus, then camp or stay at the lodge in Bartlett Cove. | no | https://www.nps.gov/glba/planyourvisit/basicinfo.htm | 2026-10-06 | “There are no roads that lead directly to Glacier Bay.” Walk-in campground and Glacier Bay Lodge at Bartlett Cove. |
| `npsa` | Reached by flights to Pago Pago on Tutuila; smaller flights continue to Ofu and Taʻū. Plan travel early. | no | https://www.nps.gov/npsa/planyourvisit/index.htm | 2026-10-06 | Accessible via flights into Pago Pago on Tutuila; “smaller flights are available to Taʻū and Ofu.” Visitors are encouraged to plan ahead. |
| `viis` | Reached by passenger ferry or car barge from St. Thomas. Camping is only at Cinnamon Bay, by reservation. | no | https://www.nps.gov/viis/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “You can either take a car barge to St. John or the people ferry.” Camping page: camping only by reservation at Cinnamon Bay. |
| `bisc` | Mainland grounds close at 5:30 p.m. Seeing the night sky here means a boat and island camping. | no | https://www.nps.gov/bisc/planyourvisit/hours.htm | 2026-10-06 | Convoy Point grounds open “7 AM-5:30 PM”. Camping page: “The only access to these islands is by boat.” |
| `wrst` | Two long gravel roads reach the park, McCarthy and Nabesna. There is no fuel along either. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/wrst/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | McCarthy Road is “a gravel road that is 59 miles long”; Nabesna Road is 42 miles; “NO FUEL options … inside the park.” |
| `dena` | One road entrance. In summer, private cars may drive only to Savage River, Mile 15 of the park road. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/dena/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “Restricted to private vehicles beyond the Savage River crossing at mile 15” in summer; one road entrance at Parks Highway mile 237. |
| `kefj` | Exit Glacier is reached by road from Seward. The fjords themselves are seen by boat or plane. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/kefj/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | Exit Glacier Road from Seward Highway mile 3.7; “Boat tours provide day trips to view the fjords.” |
| `voya` | Visitor centers are reachable by car. Nearly every campsite is reached by boat. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/voya/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | “The park's visitor centers and some trails can be reached by car”; “All campsites (except primitive) require a boat to reach.” |
| `noca` | SR 20 crosses the park but usually closes past Ross Dam Trailhead, November to April. Stehekin has no road in. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/noca/planyourvisit/directions.htm | 2026-10-06 | WSDOT “typically closes the roadway between Ross Dam Trailhead and Lone Fir Campground from November to April”; “no car access to Stehekin.” |
| `hale` | Driving into the summit between 3 and 7 a.m. needs a sunrise reservation. Overnight only in campgrounds. | yes | https://www.nps.gov/hale/planyourvisit/sunrise.htm | 2026-10-06 | Sunrise entry requires a reservation for the 3:00–7:00 AM window; stargazing page: overnight stays only in designated campgrounds. |
