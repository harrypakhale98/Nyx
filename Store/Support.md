# Nyx support

Nyx helps compare national parks and nights for stargazing. Scores are planning estimates. Confirm park access before traveling; forecasts do not cover smoke, haze or telescope seeing.

- **Clouds unavailable:** Moon, twilight and estimated skyglow still work. Refresh with a connection, or enable Cloud forecasts under Settings → Your privacy. Forecasts expire after 36 hours and nights beyond the forecast horizon have unknown clouds.
- **Access not checked:** Live alerts and programs require a publisher-provided NPS key and a successful update. Contact the park before traveling. A high score never means a road is open.
- **No nearby parks:** Widen the radius or choose a different starting park. Radius is a straight line, not driving distance.
- **Location denied:** Tap the starting-park name and choose a park. Location is optional.
- **No reminders:** Save a park and enable reminders in Settings. Only nights scoring 90+ with a complete recent cloud forecast qualify. Reminders are recalculated when Nyx opens; Apple may also suppress delivery according to notification settings.
- **AI option absent:** Optional explanations appear only when Apple's on-device model is available. Core planning does not require it.
- **Stored nights:** Journal entries and selected photos stay in the app on this iPhone. Back up your device using Apple's settings. Uninstalling can remove local records; Nyx offers no account recovery or cloud sync.

Report an issue through [Nyx on GitHub](https://github.com/harrypakhale98/Nyx/issues). Include app/iOS version, park and screen, and the steps that caused it. Do not include private observations, selected photos, precise personal location or API credentials. The owner must monitor this channel before distributing the app.
