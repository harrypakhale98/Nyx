**2/5** · `02-score.png`
> Every park, every night, gets a Darkness Score from 0 to 100.
>
> 40% moonlight (and whether the moon is up during the dark hours), 25% cloud cover, 20% light pollution, 15% length of true darkness.
>

> The calendar marks the best five-night window around each new moon.
>
