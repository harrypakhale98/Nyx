Nyx for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro

Plan a darker night.

Nyx compares the 63 US national parks and the nights ahead, and tells you where and when the sky will be darkest.

Moonlight, cloud cover, estimated light pollution and the length of true darkness become one Darkness Score, with a breakdown that explains it. Moon and twilight calculations work offline. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking.
